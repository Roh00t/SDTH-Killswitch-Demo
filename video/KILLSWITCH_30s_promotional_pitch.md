## Production Direction

The video should **not feel like seven PowerPoint slides squeezed into 30 seconds**. Treat it as one continuous visual story. Use Manim for typography, maps, tracks, network topology and UI transitions; use AI-generated footage only for the high-impact physical shots of the drone threat, Singapore environment and mobile directed-energy nodes.

Keep cuts fast during the opening problem, then slow slightly during the KILLSWITCH reveal so the product name lands. The strongest visual sequence should be **Detect → Track → Decide → Authorise → Engage**, because it communicates what the product actually does without requiring a technical explanation.

Avoid trying to explain the underlying laser physics, market size or detailed specifications in this film. The objective of these 30 seconds is to make the judges immediately understand and remember **KILLSWITCH as the coordination layer that turns distributed air-defence assets into a single human-supervised network**.
